Japan Resume (Rirekisho) Checklist

  • Photo (3×4 cm, formal)
  • Full name in kanji / romaji
  • Date of birth & age
  • Current address & phone
  • Education history (newest first)
  • Work history with company names
  • Licenses / certifications
  • Motivation / self-PR section
  • Commute time if required
  • Handwritten or clean digital format

Scam Safety Checklist

  • Does the employer ask for money (registration, training, equipment)?
    ⚠ Warning: Legitimate employers never ask candidates to pay.
  • Are they asking for bank details, My Number, or passport before an offer?
    ⚠ High risk. Share sensitive data only after a formal offer.
  • Is there extreme pressure to accept immediately or wire money?
    ⚠ Common scam tactic. Take time to verify.
  • Is the email domain free (gmail etc.) instead of a company domain?
    ⚠ Suspicious. Prefer official company email addresses.
  • Have they refused a proper interview or video call?
    ⚠ Red flag. Real companies interview candidates.

Report scams to the National Consumer Affairs Center of Japan or police if money was lost.
